Applied AI research and engineering: machine learning systems, data pipelines and the software that puts them in front of users. I judge work by measured results.

### Research interests

- **Agentic systems** - LLM agents that plan, delegate work to each other and get paid only for verified results.
- **Hardware for AI** - small NPU designs and their kernels under area and clock limits.
- **Evaluation and data** - building the measurement before the model: scoring pipelines, leaderboards, audit trails.
- **Decentralized ML** - competitive model and design markets on Bittensor.

### Approach

Start from a real problem, write the measurement first, test many ideas cheaply and keep what the evidence supports. Failed experiments stay on record with their results, so nobody repeats them.
